A half-day trip to Sarnath from Varanasi

Where the Buddha gave his first sermon. Easy to fit into an afternoon from our colony in Kamachha.

2–3 hrsat Sarnath, plus travel
Frithe museum is closed on Fridays
10–5museum hours

What to see

  • Dhamek Stupa, the large stupa marking the site of the first sermon.
  • The excavated monastery ruins and the Ashoka pillar base around the stupa.
  • Sarnath Museum, home of the Ashoka lion capital, India's national emblem. Open 10 am to 5 pm, closed on Fridays.
  • Mulagandha Kuti Vihara, a modern temple with murals of the Buddha's life.
  • The Thai, Japanese and Tibetan temples nearby, each in its own style.

Plan it like this

  1. Leave after lunch, by about 1:30 pm.
  2. See the museum first, before it closes at 5 pm.
  3. Walk the stupa and ruins in the late-afternoon light.
  4. Stop at one or two of the temples, then head back for dinner.

Sarnath is north of the city, so allow time for traffic. A cab is the easiest option; your host can help you plan the route.

Questions

How much time do I need in Sarnath?

Two to three hours covers the stupa, ruins, museum and a temple or two, plus travel time.

Is the Sarnath Museum open on Fridays?

No. The Sarnath Museum is closed on Fridays. It opens from 10 am to 5 pm on other days.

Can I do Sarnath and the Ganga aarti on the same day?

Yes, if you leave for Sarnath around midday and head to the ghats by about 5 pm in winter.
