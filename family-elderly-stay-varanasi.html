Families and elders

A Varanasi stay that works for families and elderly guests

All six rooms are on the ground floor with a private bathroom, in a quiet residential colony. No stairs, no noise, and a kitchen in five rooms.

Why families choose us

  • Ground floor only. Easy for elders with walking sticks, heavy bags and small children.
  • Private bathrooms with hot water. Every room has its own bathroom with a geyser.
  • Your own kitchen. Make tea, khichdi or a light meal for elders and kids on your own schedule.
  • Car to the gate. The road is about 20 ft wide and car-accessible up to the gate, so you are not dragging bags through lanes.
  • A host who checks in. Your host checks in with you during the stay and can help with temple timings and directions.

Room picks for families

Each room is priced for 2 guests and takes 1 extra person at an extra charge (up to 3 per room). Larger families usually book two rooms side by side.

Tips for temple visits with elders

  • Go to Kashi Vishwanath on a weekday morning, when queues are usually shorter. See our darshan and aarti timings.
  • Plan one big outing a day and rest in the afternoon.
  • Carry water, a small bag and comfortable footwear.

Questions

Are there stairs?

No. All six rooms are on the ground floor.

How many people can stay in one room?

Each price is for 2 guests, and 1 extra person can stay at an extra charge, so up to 3 per room. Families often book two rooms.

Can children stay?

Yes. Guests under 18 are welcome with a parent or guardian.

Can a car reach the property?

Yes. The road is car-accessible up to the gate, with free street parking in the colony.
