About the King AC Suite

This is the biggest room at Mahadev Niwas. It has a single 6×6 ft king bed, AC, a private kitchen with induction and utensils, a full-length mirror and a wall projector with speakers. The private bathroom has a geyser for hot water.

Couples like it for the space and the movie nights; small families like that everyone fits without feeling cramped. Like every room here, it is on the ground floor, so there are no stairs.

Where you are

Mahadev Niwas is a family-run homestay in a quiet residential colony in Kamachha, Varanasi. Assi Ghat is about 2.5 km by road, and the ghats are 15–20 minutes away by cab or scooty. The road is car-accessible up to the gate, with free street parking in the colony and paid parking next door if you need it.

House rules in short

  • Only booked guests stay; no outside visitors
  • Quiet hours 10 pm to 7 am
  • Guests under 18 only with a parent or guardian
  • Smoking is allowed in the room; please use the ashtray
  • No pets
