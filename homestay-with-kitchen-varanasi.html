Why a kitchen helps in Varanasi

Many guests come for a few days of temple visits and ghat walks, with early starts and late evenings. Being able to make tea at 4 am before Mangla Aarti, or a light dinner after a long day, saves time and money and keeps food simple for elders and children.

What the kitchens are set up for

  • An induction cooktop, basic utensils and cups
  • Tea, coffee, Maggi, eggs, sabzi-roti and other light, simple cooking
  • A shared fridge and RO drinking water for all guests

The kitchens are made for light cooking. For heavy frying or large meals, we can point you to good local veg restaurants.

Groceries nearby

Kamachha and Bhelupur have everyday grocery shops, fruit and vegetable sellers and dairies a short ride away. Your host can tell you where to go.

Questions

Do the rooms have a kitchen?

Yes. Rooms 1, 3, 4 and 6 have a private kitchen and Room 5 has a kitchenette. Room 2, our budget room, has no kitchen but has a kettle.

Is the kitchen shared?

No. Each kitchen belongs to its room. Only the fridge and RO drinking water are shared.

Can I cook non-veg food?

Please ask us on WhatsApp before you book, and we will tell you what is possible.

Is there a fridge?

Yes, there is a common fridge for all guests, and RO drinking water.
