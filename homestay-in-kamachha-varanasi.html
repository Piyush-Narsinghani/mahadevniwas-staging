Where we are in Kamachha

Mahadev Niwas is in Ganpati Dham Colony (Kolhua, Vinayaka), a quiet residential lane off the Rathyatra–Kamachha road in the Bhelupur area of Varanasi. Our full address is N-16/95A-29B, Ganpati Dham Colony, Kamachha, Varanasi 221010, opposite Rewat Apartment-2. For drivers, the easiest landmark is Surya Vishal Complex.

Why stay in a residential colony

  • Quiet nights. No ghat-side crowds, loudspeakers or late-night traffic outside your window.
  • Easy access. The road is about 20 ft wide and car-accessible up to the gate, not a narrow gali.
  • Free parking on the street in the colony, with paid parking next door if you need it.
  • Everyday shops, dairies and fruit sellers a short ride away in Kamachha and Bhelupur.

Questions

Is Kamachha a good area to stay in Varanasi?

Yes, if you want quiet nights. Kamachha is a residential area in the Bhelupur side of the city, with local shops and food nearby, and the ghats are 15–20 minutes away by cab.

How far is Mahadev Niwas from the ghats?

Assi Ghat is about 2.5 km and Kashi Vishwanath temple about 3.5 km away, usually 15–20 minutes by cab.

Can a car reach the homestay?

Yes. Our road is about 20 ft wide and cars can come right up to the gate. Free street parking is available in the colony.

What landmark should I give my driver?

Surya Vishal Complex, Kamachha. We are in Ganpati Dham Colony, opposite Rewat Apartment-2, and we guide your driver on the phone.

How do I get around from Kamachha?

Cabs, Uber and Ola are easy to get here, and we can connect you with a local scooty rental vendor.
