Booking policy

How booking directly with us works.

How booking works

  1. Verify your email with Google or a one-time email link. We do not accept unverified requests.
  2. Send your request with dates, guests, your name and WhatsApp number. No payment is taken on the website.
  3. We confirm on WhatsApp with the final price for your dates.
  4. Pay 100% in advance by UPI to hold the room. Your booking is confirmed once payment is received.

No cancellations

  • All direct bookings are final and non-refundable.
  • Bookings cannot be cancelled once paid, and no refund is given for no-shows, late arrival or early departure.
  • Please check your dates and travel plans carefully before you pay.

Guests and house rules

  • Prices are for 2 guests; each room allows 1 extra person at an extra charge.
  • Valid photo ID for every adult at check-in. Local Varanasi IDs are not accepted.
  • Only booked guests stay; no outside visitors. Quiet hours 10 pm to 7 am.
  • Guests under 18 only with a parent or guardian. No pets.
  • Self check-in from 12:00 noon, checkout by 10:00 am. Late checkout needs an extra night booked.

Your data

We keep your name, email, WhatsApp number and booking dates only to manage your stay, and we never sell or share them. Ask us on WhatsApp at any time to delete your details.
