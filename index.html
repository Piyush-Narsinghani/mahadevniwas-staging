Getting around

A quiet colony, a short ride from the ghats

Questions

How far are the ghats and Kashi Vishwanath?

Assi Ghat is about 2.5 km and Kashi Vishwanath Temple about 3 km by road: 15 to 20 minutes by cab or scooty.

How many guests does the price include?

Every price is for 2 guests. Each room allows 1 extra person, at an extra charge.

Is smoking allowed?

Yes, smoking is allowed inside the rooms. Please use the ashtray and keep the room clean.

Do the rooms have a kitchen?

Rooms 1, 3, 4 and 6 have a private kitchen and Room 5 has a kitchenette. Room 2 has no kitchen.

What are the check-in and checkout times?

Self check-in from 12:00 noon. Checkout by 10:00 am. Late checkout needs an extra night booked.

Can I get an extra mattress?

Yes, for an additional charge. Ask on WhatsApp before you arrive.

Is Mahadev Niwas good for families and elderly guests?

Yes. All six rooms are on the ground floor with a private bathroom, so there are no stairs. Every room fits up to 3 guests, and five rooms have a kitchen or kitchenette.

Do you have a budget room in Varanasi?

Yes. Room 2 is our lowest-priced room: a fan room with a private bathroom, hot water and a kettle.

Is there parking?

There is free street parking in the colony. If you need it, paid parking can be arranged next door.

How do I book?

Every room is booked through Airbnb. Pick a room above and tap Check dates on Airbnb. For questions, message us on WhatsApp.
